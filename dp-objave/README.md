# DP objave

Instagram posts and stories in the Druga perspektiva style, made inside WordPress instead of Canva.

- **From an article:** in Članci, hover an article and click **Napravi objavu** (or use the "Instagram" box on the article's edit screen). The headline, category chip and its colour, featured photo, photo credit ("Foto: …" from the image caption), first paragraph and pull quotes fill in by themselves.
- **From scratch:** Objave za mreže → Dodaj novu.
- **Slides:** Naslovna (photo, chip, headline), Tekst (bold line and paragraph on a colour), Citat (quote with the orange line). Add, copy, reorder and delete slides; a carousel is just several slides.
- **Formats:** Objava 4:5 (1080 × 1350) and Story 9:16 (1080 × 1920).
- **Photos:** 1 to 3 per cover (stacked), from the Media Library or uploaded on the spot. Drag the photo in the preview to choose what shows; zoom, darkening behind the headline and blur are sliders.
- **Download:** "Preuzmi ovaj slajd" or "Preuzmi sve" saves PNG files in exactly Instagram's size, with the site's fonts (Lato) and the DP logo.
- **Saved:** every design is kept under Objave za mreže, so it can be opened and changed later. They never appear on the public site.

Fonts: Lato (SIL Open Font License), bundled in `assets/fonts`.
