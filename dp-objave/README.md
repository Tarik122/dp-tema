# DP objave

Instagram posts and stories in the Druga perspektiva style, made inside WordPress instead of Canva.

- **From an article:** in Članci, hover an article and click **Napravi objavu** (or use the "Instagram" box on the article's edit screen). The headline, category chip and its colour, author ("Piše: …"), featured photo, photo credit ("Foto: …" from the image caption), first paragraph and pull quotes fill in by themselves.
- **From scratch:** Objave za mreže → Dodaj novu.
- **Slides:**
  - **Naslovna:** full-bleed photo, smooth black gradient at the bottom, chip, heavy headline (Lato Black), author, photo credit, logo.
  - **Tekst:** bold line and paragraph on a colour. With the paragraph left empty it becomes a big condensed statement, like a poster.
  - **Citat:** a big condensed quote (Barlow Condensed) with Bosnian quote marks, the name in bold and the description in grey, Apple News style.
  - Add, copy, reorder and delete slides; a carousel is just several slides.
- **Styles:** one switch changes the whole design:
  - **DP klasik:** the current Instagram look (big chip, Lato Bold, purple text slides, orange line on quotes).
  - **Apple:** small kicker, tight Lato Bold headlines, big condensed quotes on light grey.
  - **Magazin:** Source Serif headlines, italic subtitle and italic quotes, like the website.
- **Formats:** Objava 3:4 (Instagram's new grid) and Story 9:16. Downloads are double resolution (2160 × 2880 and 2160 × 3840), which survives Instagram's compression better.
- **Photos:** one photo per cover (a 2–3 photo collage is optional), from the Media Library or uploaded on the spot. Drag the photo in the preview to choose what shows; zoom, the strength of the bottom gradient and blur are sliders.
- **Download:** "Preuzmi ovaj slajd" or "Preuzmi sve" saves PNG files in exactly Instagram's size, with the site's fonts (Lato) and the DP logo.
- **Saved:** every design is kept under Objave za mreže, so it can be opened and changed later. They never appear on the public site.

Fonts: Lato, Barlow Condensed and Source Serif 4 (all SIL Open Font License), bundled in `assets/fonts`.
